 # Zotero

Zotero is an open-source software for academic references. It allows you to create a list of references and add them to your academic paper with a click. Zotero is published under GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3). It is constantly updated; as of the moment I am writing (November 23rd), the last modifications happened just 15 hours ago. There are around twenty people who have actively contributed to its GitHub in the last year. There are around 2600 issues closed on the GitHub page, with the last being closed around three weeks ago. Zotero is now self-funded by users, but it was created “at the Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media at George Mason University with initial funding from the Andrew W. Mellon Foundation, the United States Institute of Museum and Library Services, and the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation”.
